Required Documents

Please keep the following documents ready before your meeting with our team

Salaried Employees
  1. Last 6 months salary slip (Original)
  2. Bank statements for 1 year (Salary Account)
  3. Pan card, I card, Aadhar card Color xerox
  4. Appointment & Confirmation Letter, Form No.16 Last 2 yrs
  5. PF Statement (For Government Employee)
  6. Residential Proof (Ration Card, Light bill)
  7. Qualification Certificate, 3 Colour Photo
  8. All loan details & Statement
  9. Co applicant (Pan card, Aadhar card Color, Photos 3)
  10. Cheque 1
Business Profile
  1. Last 3yrs ITR C.A Attested (Statement of income, Profit & Loss Ac, Balance sheet, Capital Ac, Tax paid challan), Form 26AS
  2. Bank statements for 1 year (Saving & Current Account)
  3. Pan card, Aadhar card Color xerox
  4. Gumsta License, Udyog Aadhar, Shop Agreement, Light bill copy
  5. Business Profile in company letter head
  6. Residential Proof (Ration Card, Light bill)
  7. Qualification Certificate, 3 Color Photo
  8. All loan details & Statement
  9. Co applicant (Pan card, Aadhar card color, Photos 3)
  10. Cheque
Property Document (Builder)
  • Builder Legal Documents (Development Agr. / Partnership Deed / Power of Attorney / 7/12 / Extrac / Title / Search Copy / Blue Print Plan Copy)
  • Sale Agreement Original / Draft Agreement
  • Builder NOC Favour of Bank (Format), Demand & Architect Letter
  • OCR Receipt & Bank Clearance
Property Document – Resale / Mortgage / LAP
  • Purchase Agreement Copy / Chain of Agreement
  • Tax, Maintenance, Light Bill Latest Copy
  • Share Certificate
  • Society Registration Certificate
  • OC Copy, Plan Copy
  • Society NOC Favour of Bank (Format), OCR with Bank Clearance
  • Seller Pan Card / Aadhar Card / Photo / Cancelled Cheque / Request Letter
Balance Transfer + Top Up
  • List of Documents, Outstanding Letter & Track Report
